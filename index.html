Rupesh Prasad Kushwaha works as a Health Assistant (HA) at A2Z Hospital Pvt. What I do

Areas of clinical support

The core responsibilities I handle daily as a Health Assistant to keep patient care smooth and reliable.

🩺

General health checkups

Recording vitals — blood pressure, temperature, pulse and oxygen levels — for accurate diagnosis.

💊

Medication assistance

Helping patients understand dosage schedules and supporting doctors with medicine administration.

🧑‍⚕️

Doctor & nurse support

Assisting the clinical team during examinations, minor procedures, and ward rounds.

📋

Patient record keeping

Maintaining accurate treatment history and health records for continuous, informed care.

🚑

Emergency response support

Providing first-line assistance during emergencies until the treating doctor takes over.

📢

Health awareness camps

Organizing and supporting community outreach programs on hygiene, nutrition, and prevention.
